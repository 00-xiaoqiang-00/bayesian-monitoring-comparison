Trial / article
===============
Trial name: KEYNOTE-598
Registration: NCT03302234
Article: Pembrolizumab Plus Ipilimumab or Placebo for Metastatic Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer With PD-L1 Tumor Proportion Score >= 50%: Randomized, Double-Blind Phase III KEYNOTE-598 Study
Population: Intention-to-treat population; previously untreated metastatic NSCLC with PD-L1 tumor proportion score >= 50% and no sensitizing EGFR/ALK alteration
Experimental group: Pembrolizumab + ipilimumab (n = 284)
Control group: Pembrolizumab + placebo (n = 284)
Data cutoff: September 1, 2020
Median follow-up: 20.6 months (range, 12.4-31.7)

The CSV files are headerless. Column 1 is time in months and column 2 is the
survival probability on the 0-1 scale. Curves were digitized from Figure 2A
(OS) and Figure 2C (PFS) using exact vector-rendered line colors at 600 dpi,
with dense horizontal-pixel sampling, censor-mark suppression, and monotonicity
enforcement.
